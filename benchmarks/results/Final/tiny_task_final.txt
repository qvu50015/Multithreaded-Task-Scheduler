Tiny Task Benchmark — Final Results
===================================

Environment
-----------
Platform: Apple Silicon Mac
Language Standard: C++20
Workers tested: 1, 2, 4, 8
Runs per configuration: 10

Workload
--------
100,000 extremely small tasks
Each task performs a simple multiplication.
Timing unit: microseconds

Results
-------
Sequential
Average: 336.1 us
Slowdown: 1.00x

ThreadPool (1 worker)
Average: 111,141 us
Slowdown: 330.679x

ThreadPool (2 workers)
Average: 111,844 us
Slowdown: 332.769x

ThreadPool (4 workers)
Average: 112,702 us
Slowdown: 335.324x

ThreadPool (8 workers)
Average: 108,909 us
Slowdown: 324.037x

Summary
-------
Extremely fine-grained tasks are dominated by scheduler overhead.

Task creation, futures, synchronization, queue operations, task
submission, scheduling, and result collection cost far more than the
computation performed by each individual task.

This demonstrates that parallel execution is most effective when tasks
contain enough useful computation to amortize scheduling overhead.

Timing includes task submission, scheduling, execution, and future
result collection, but excludes ThreadPool construction.
