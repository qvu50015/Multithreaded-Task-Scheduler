Uneven Task Benchmark — Final Results
=====================================

Environment
-----------
Platform: Apple Silicon Mac
Language Standard: C++20
Workers tested: 1, 2, 4, 8
Runs per configuration: 10

Workload
--------
16 tasks with deliberately uneven computational workloads
Iteration counts range from 1,000,000 to 90,000,000
Total workload: 555,000,000 iterations

Results
-------
Sequential
Average: 444.4 ms
Speedup: 1.00x

ThreadPool (1 worker)
Average: 443.8 ms
Speedup: 1.00135x

ThreadPool (2 workers)
Average: 237.9 ms
Speedup: 1.86801x

ThreadPool (4 workers)
Average: 133.8 ms
Speedup: 3.32138x

ThreadPool (8 workers)
Average: 100.5 ms
Speedup: 4.42189x

Summary
-------
The scheduler achieved a 4.42x speedup with 8 workers on a deliberately
uneven workload.

Per-worker task counts and workload measurements showed that workers
executed different numbers of tasks as idle workers stole work from
other queues.

This demonstrates dynamic load balancing through per-worker queues and
work stealing.
