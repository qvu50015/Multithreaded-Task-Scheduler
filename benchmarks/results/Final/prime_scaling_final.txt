Summary
-------
The scheduler achieved a 4.46x speedup with 8 workers compared with
sequential execution.

Scaling remained strong through four workers. At eight workers,
execution time continued to improve while efficiency decreased due to
scheduling overhead, synchronization, contention, and limited available
parallelism.
