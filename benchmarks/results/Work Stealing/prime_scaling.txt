Prime Scaling Benchmark — Work Stealing

Workload:
Count primes from 2 to 5,000,000.
16 tasks.
5 runs per configuration.

Sequential:
Average: 731.0 ms

Thread Pool Results:
Workers: 1
Average: 721.2 ms
Speedup: 1.01359x
Efficiency: 101.359%

Workers: 2
Average: 380.0 ms
Speedup: 1.92368x
Efficiency: 96.1842%

Workers: 4
Average: 218.4 ms
Speedup: 3.34707x
Efficiency: 83.6767%

Workers: 8
Average: 167.8 ms
Speedup: 4.35638x
Efficiency: 54.4547%

Interpretation:
The scheduler scales strongly for CPU-bound computational tasks.
Performance improves from approximately 1.01x with one worker to
4.36x with eight workers.

Parallel efficiency remains high at two and four workers before
decreasing at eight workers due to scheduling overhead, contention,
and limited available parallelism.

The slightly greater than 100% efficiency with one worker is
attributable to normal benchmark timing variation.
