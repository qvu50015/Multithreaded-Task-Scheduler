Interpretation:
The scheduler dynamically redistributes uneven workloads across
workers.

Workers execute different numbers of tasks because task durations
vary significantly. Work stealing allows workers that finish their
local work to execute tasks from other worker queues.

The uneven workload benchmark achieved:
1.88x speedup with 2 workers
3.43x speedup with 4 workers
4.26x speedup with 8 workers

The worker-level workload measurements show substantially more
balanced computational work than a static assignment would provide,
demonstrating the scheduler's ability to adapt to load imbalance.
