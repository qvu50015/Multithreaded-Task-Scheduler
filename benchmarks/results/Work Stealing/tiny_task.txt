Tiny Task Benchmark — Work Stealing

Workload:
100,000 extremely small tasks.
Each task computes a simple multiplication.
5 runs per configuration.

Sequential:
Average: 206 µs

Thread Pool Results:
Workers: 1
Average: 120717 µs
Slowdown: 586.003x

Workers: 2
Average: 119793 µs
Slowdown: 581.517x

Workers: 4
Average: 113187 µs
Slowdown: 549.452x

Workers: 8
Average: 106343 µs
Slowdown: 516.230x

Interpretation:
Extremely fine-grained tasks are dominated by scheduler overhead.

Although increasing the worker count reduces total thread-pool
execution time, all thread-pool configurations remain substantially
slower than direct sequential execution.

The cost of queue operations, synchronization, futures, task
submission, and scheduling is much larger than the computational
work performed by each individual task.

This demonstrates that task granularity is an important consideration
when using a multithreaded task scheduler.
