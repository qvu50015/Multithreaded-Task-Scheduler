Prime Scaling Benchmark
=======================

Environment
-----------
CPU: Apple M3
Compiler: C++20
Workers tested: 1, 2, 4, 8

Workload
--------
Type: CPU-bound prime counting
Range: 2 -> 5,000,000
Number of tasks: 16
Runs per configuration: 5

Correctness
-----------
Expected prime count: 348,513
All benchmark runs produced the expected result.

Summary
-------
Sequential baseline: 726.0 ms

1 worker:  720.2 ms  | 1.01x speedup
2 workers: 381.2 ms  | 1.90x speedup
4 workers: 208.0 ms  | 3.49x speedup
8 workers: 161.6 ms  | 4.49x speedup

Best result:
8 workers achieved a 4.49x speedup compared with the sequential
baseline for this CPU-bound prime-counting workload.

Scaling observation:
Performance improved as the number of worker threads increased,
but scaling was not linear. The improvement from 4 to 8 workers
was smaller than the improvement from 2 to 4 workers, indicating
diminishing returns at higher worker counts.
